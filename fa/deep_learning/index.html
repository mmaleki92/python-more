Introduction to Deep Learning

Deep learning is a subset of machine learning where artificial neural networks, algorithms inspired by the human brain, learn from large amounts of data. These networks can identify patterns and make decisions with minimal human intervention.

Key Concepts in Deep Learning

Neurons

The basic computational units that receive inputs, apply an activation function, and produce outputs.

Weights & Biases

Parameters that determine the strength of connections between neurons and are adjusted during training.

Activation Functions

Mathematical functions that determine the output of a neuron, introducing non-linearity to the network.

Backpropagation

The algorithm used to calculate gradients and update weights during training to minimize error.

Convolutional Networks

Specialized neural networks designed for processing grid-like data, such as images.

Recurrent Networks

Networks with feedback connections, allowing them to maintain memory of past inputs.
